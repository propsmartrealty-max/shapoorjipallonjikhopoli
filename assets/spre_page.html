Connected To Today.
Aligned With Tomorrow.

Strategically positioned between Mumbai and Pune, Khopoli is emerging as a destination shaped by infrastructure, opportunity and natural surroundings. Improved connectivity through the Mumbai–Pune Expressway and the newly operational Connecting Link is bringing the region closer to both cities. With the larger Mumbai 3.0 vision and Navi Mumbai International Airport adding momentum to the region, Khopoli is becoming part of an evolving growth corridor.

Beyond its connectivity lies something increasingly rare, expansive natural landscapes, the quiet of the Sahyadris and room to experience life differently. It is where the promise of growth meets the value of space, nature and a more considered way of living.

Located in Khopoli, with connectivity to Mumbai, Pune the project is strategically located in emerging Mumbai 3.0 growth corridor, powered by major infrastructure investment and strong market momentum.

Positioned amidst natural landscapes while remaining connected to major road and rail infrastructure, it offers a unique balance of accessibility and escape.
